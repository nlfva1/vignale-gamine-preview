French Brochure

The French-market sales brochure for the Vignale Gamine.

Original French brochure text (translated):

The GAMINE in life is a different life — a reminder of the belle époque — an invitation to relax — permanent contact with nature. On the beach, in the mountains, in town, she is always “in the wind” [in fashion], for the young... and the not-so-young. With the GAMINE, you also outwit traffic problems. She slips through traffic jams — in car parks, easy to manoeuvre, she parks in the smallest of spaces, because besides her reduced footprint, her front bumpers are designed for “Parking”. The GAMINE, finally, is also a second car... or one that might very well be taken for one.
