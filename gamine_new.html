What is New at this Site ?

A running log of updates since the site began in 1997 — new Gamines, brochures, stories and finds, newest first.

  • August 2026

    New, mobile-friendly website design — now available in six languages (English, Dutch, Italian, Spanish, French, German). · The ‘Virtual Vignale Gamine Club’ has been renamed to the Worldwide Gamine Register. · Updated register count: 824 Gamines in 38 countries.

  • August 2024

    Vignale Gamine book by Guus C.M. Beliën. · Link to the Facebook group ‘I proprietari di Vignale Gamine (110F Spider)’.

  • April 2021 (updated August 2024)

    Sorry Gamine and Vignale enthusiasts: nothing much new on the site since over a decade… BUT: with help from owners in the UK, France, Netherlands, Germany, Italy and Switzerland, we were able to find 540 Gamines in 29 countries! Of course, when Gamines get exported, they might be double in the Register — so we DO need your help: please fill out the PDF of the Register, include your chassis number, and send it to us. So far, we have 245 chassis numbers already! Update 08-2024: 761 Gamines in 34 countries + 441 unique VINs!
